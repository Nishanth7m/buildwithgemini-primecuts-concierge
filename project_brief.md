# My agent: PrimeCuts - Fresh Chicken & Mutton Gourmet Concierge

One-liner: A conversational agent that helps home cooks and food enthusiasts choose premium cuts of fresh chicken and mutton, learn recipe pairings, order temperature-controlled deliveries, and calculate portions/nutrition with a catalog of artisan poultry and goat meat cuts.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers customer preferences (e.g. spice tolerance, family portion sizes, preferred cuts, dietary goals like high-protein or bone-in preferences, past delivery address).
- Tools: Look up cuts by cooking style, check real-time cold-chain delivery slots, place orders, track live dispatch status, and suggest culinary marinades.
- Catalog/UI: Rich A2UI cards displaying meat cuts (500g curry cuts, biryani cuts, boneless breasts, chops, keema) with prices, freshness ratings, and recipe tags.
- Image gen: Generates mouthwatering visual plating previews (e.g., Dum Biryani plating, sizzling Chaap roast, grilled herb chicken breast).
- Sandbox: Computes custom marinade ratios, cooking durations based on meat weight, and macro/protein breakdown per portion.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for meat portioning & macro calculations, Maps API for live delivery routing, Vertex AI Memory Bank for long-term customer preference recall.
